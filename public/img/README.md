# Placeholder photography — NOT FOR LAUNCH

Every image in this folder is a stand-in pulled from Unsplash to design against,
at the client's request ("use dummy data and pictures from online"). They are
free to use under the Unsplash licence, but **none of them is Indrakosh**:

- No image shows an Indrakosh product, showroom, workshop or person.
- The interiors are European, not Tamil Nadu vernacular.
- `maker.webp` is a stranger. It must not ship as "our craftsman".
- The Brand Guidelines explicitly forbid "generic or overly staged stock images",
  so this whole folder is on borrowed time by design.

Replace with a real shoot before anyone outside the project sees this: showroom
interiors, materials at raking light, the sawmill, hands working, the founder.

## Excluded on purpose

- `sawing.webp` — carries a legible third-party tool brand across the blade and
  is high-key B&W, which punches a white hole in a dark warm page.
- `brass-hook.webp` — shot against a bright green garden; one high-key frame in
  a row of dark ones reads as a hole, and no CSS filter rescues a blown white.

Both are kept in the folder only so the reasoning above stays checkable.

## Grading

Images are graded in CSS, not baked: `.backdrop .plate img` and `.ch-media img`
carry saturate/contrast/brightness/sepia filters. That is what makes twenty
photographs from twenty photographers read as one art direction — and it means
swapping in the real shoot does not require re-editing every file.
